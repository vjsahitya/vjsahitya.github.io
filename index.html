Our Story

We met during our first job in Hyderabad.

What started as two people working in the same city slowly became a six-year story.

Then COVID happened.

For two years, we learnt how to love across distance, through screens, calls, waiting and countless little moments.

And then came the mountains.

Sahitya proposed while we were trekking to Tungnath, surrounded by the hills, with our closest people secretly involved in the surprise.

The proposal happened close to Shiva — a moment that felt incredibly meant to be.

Now, after six years together, we're beginning our next chapter.
