OUR STORY

Six years in the making

HYDERABAD

Where it began

We met during our first job in Hyderabad — two people learning to work, and quietly, learning each other. What started as familiar faces in the same city slowly became a six-year story neither of us saw coming.

TWO CITIES, ONE SCREEN

The years apart

Then came a season none of us expected. For two years we learnt how to love across distance — through screens, calls, waiting, and the countless small moments that quietly held us together.

TUNGNATH, THE HIMALAYAS

The proposal

And then came the mountains. Sahitya proposed on the trek to Tungnath, the hills standing witness and our closest people secretly in on it — close to Shiva, in a moment that felt entirely meant to be.

NOW

Our next chapter

Six years together, and we're only just beginning. We can't wait to celebrate this next chapter of our forever with the people who mean the most to us.
